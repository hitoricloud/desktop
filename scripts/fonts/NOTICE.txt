This is a flags-only WOFF2 subset (U+1F1E6–U+1F1FF, with shaping closure).
COLRv1 color glyphs are retained. The SVG table is omitted for Chromium compatibility.
The characters in the interface remain Unicode emoji; no image substitution is used.
